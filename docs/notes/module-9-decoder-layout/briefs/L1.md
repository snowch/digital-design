# L1: where four decoder figures open, and how a learner sets K, J and C

Draft replacement sentences for four figure leads in lessons 9.1 and 9.2. Edit against
`docs/style.md` (attached): British English, short sentences, active voice, the learner is "you",
no em dashes, no filler. Write only the replacement sentences asked for, each marked with its
letter. Keep every fact below; add none.

Facts about the figures, checked against the code:

- A figure that draws a circuit has a trail above the drawing when a block can be opened. The
  course already says: "The trail above the drawing names each level; press a name to go back."
  Reuse that sentence where a going-back sentence is asked for.
- K, J and C are words of 4, 4 and 12 bits. Their pins in the drawing cannot be pressed. Under the
  drawing there is a row of bit boxes for each word, headed "Input K", "Input J" and "Input C".
  Pressing a bit changes it.
- Those rows show only at the figure's top level, where the decoder is drawn closed. Inside a
  block they are gone.

A. Lesson 9.1, figure "decoder-open". It opens at the top level, the decoder closed. The lead ends:
   "Press a block to open it. [SENTENCE A] The figure starts with a subtract: kind 1, job 3."
   SENTENCE A replaces "Press K's and J's pins to change the instruction." It must say: you change
   the instruction with the bit boxes under the drawing, in the rows for K and J; the rows show only
   at the top level; to get back there, use the trail (the sentence above). Two or three sentences.

B. Lesson 9.1, figure "decoder-faults", a fault lab. It now opens inside the decoder's "control
   signals" block. The lead begins: "[SENTENCE B] Each fault breaks one gate, or one line into the
   block." SENTENCE B replaces "The figure shows the decoder: press it, then its control signals
   block." It must say the figure opens inside the decoder's "control signals" block, then the trail
   sentence. Two sentences.

C. Lesson 9.2, figure "checks-open". It now opens inside the decoder's "checks" block.
   C1 replaces the lead's first sentence, "Press the decoder in the figure, then its "checks"
   block." It must say the figure opens inside the decoder's "checks" block. One sentence. The
   next sentence is "The instruction is `82000005`: kind 8, job 2, constant 5."
   C2 replaces the lead's last sentence, "Press the pins for K, J and C to try other words." The
   sentence before it is "Press a block to open it." C2 must say: to try other words, go back to
   the top level with the trail; there, set K, J and C with the bit boxes under the drawing. Two or
   three sentences.

D. Lesson 9.2, figure "check-faults", a fault lab. It now opens inside the decoder's "checks"
   block. SENTENCE D replaces "The figure shows the decoder: press it, then its "checks" block."
   The next sentence is "Each fault breaks one check." One sentence.
