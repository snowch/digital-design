Read `docs/style.md` first. The first draft read "from {from} to {to}, writing {transfer}". It is
shown as a whole line, a status line under a figure's buttons, and the lane names are capitalised
as headings ("The handler", "Main program", "Run 2's first program"), so a line that begins
"from The handler" reads wrongly.

Key: `lanes.move`. Facts: `{from}` and `{to}` are two lanes' names, each beginning with a
capital; `{transfer}` is what the move writes, such as `PC ← 010` or `C2 ← 0FC, C3 ← 32`. The
line says the run moved from the first lane to the second, and what that move wrote. Begin with
`{from}`, so the capital comes first. Keep the three placeholders exactly. No full stop. No
backticks.
