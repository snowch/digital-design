# Brief Y2: the review of the figures pass, Module 11 (lessons 2 to 5)

Read `docs/style.md` first. Module 11's terms each lesson may use: lesson 2 `lists`: breakpoint;
lesson 3 `functions`: function, arguments, calling convention; lesson 4 `stack`: stack; lesson 5
`recursion`: recursion. No term from Module 12. Write each key from its facts alone. Answer in the
form `key: text`. A key marked "(replaces ...)" gives the exact words it replaces: write only the
new words. Code names (addresses, lines, registers in code) go in backticks in prose, as the
existing text does; never in a caption or a label.

Where the drawings sit: under the program's listing on a wide screen; on a narrow screen, after
the watch in lesson 3 and after the stack in lesson 5. Do not say "under the debugger".

## lesson `stack` (4)

- `pushedLead.drawing` (replaces the last paragraph of the lead: "The stack is drawn as boxes,
  from `7B8` down. Each word is marked with the register whose word it saves. The words one call
  pushed are grouped under that call's name and the place it was called from. R14's arrow points
  at the word on top.") Facts:
  - the stack is drawn as boxes, R14's word first, at the top; the words further down the drawing
    were pushed earlier;
  - each box is marked with the register whose word it saves;
  - R14's arrow points at the word on top; between a push's two lines, after R14 has gone down and
    before the store, the arrow points at an address with no box yet, because no push has stored a
    word there;
  - a word a pop has passed stays drawn, dashed, set apart above the stack under its own heading:
    it is no longer on the stack, but it is still in the RAM.
  Do not describe the grouping by call: the explanation does.
- `captions.pushed` — a caption: `sumOver` with a stack, in the debugger, with the stack drawn as
  boxes. No backticks. A sentence fragment ending in a full stop.

## lesson `recursion` (5)

- `roomsLead.drawing` (replaces the second paragraph of the question's lead: "Below the words, the
  same rooms are drawn from those words. Each room is a box with its name and reading. A door to a
  room is joined to that room by a line, drawn below and to the right. A door that leads nowhere is
  drawn as a circle with 0.") Facts: below the words, the same rooms are drawn from those words; a
  key under the drawing says how to read it. One sentence.
- `boxes.roomsKeyStill` — the key under the question's drawing, which has no run. Facts: each room
  is a box with its name and reading. Its two doors are circles to its right: a circle with 1 or 2
  is a door to a room; a dashed circle with 0 is a door that leads nowhere. Each room behind a door
  is drawn below, a step to the right, and a line runs to it from the box of the room it opens
  from. Do not mention R1, a call, or anything lit.
- `framesLead.drawing` (replaces the last paragraph of the investigation's lead: "The drawing of
  the rooms lights the room whose address R1 holds, the room the current call of `warmRooms` is
  about.") Facts:
  - at each pause, R1 holds the address of the room the new call is about, and the drawing lights
    that room;
  - at the seven pauses where R1 holds 0, the call came through a door that leads nowhere. R10
    still holds the calling room's address, and R15 holds `060` after a call through the first
    door, `070` after a call through the second. The drawing marks that door of that room;
  - between pauses the light follows R1, not the call.
  Say where the drawing sits (see above). Three or four short sentences.
- `boxes.roomsKey` — the key under the investigation's drawing. Facts, without repeating the lead's
  reasons: the lit room is the room whose address R1 holds; each room is a box with its name and
  reading; its two doors are circles to its right, a circle with 1 or 2 a door to a room, a dashed

  call came through, shown when R1 holds 0; each room behind a door is drawn below, a step to the
  right, and a line runs to it from the box of the room it opens from.
- `boxes.roomLit` — said after the drawing's name to a screen reader: `{name}` is lit; R1 holds its

- `boxes.doorFollowed` — said after it: door `{door}` of `{name}` is marked; R1 holds 0, and the
  current call came through that door. One or two short sentences, keep `{door}` and `{name}`.
- `captions.frames` — a caption: the calls of `warmRooms`, the stack and the rooms, in the
  debugger. No backticks.
