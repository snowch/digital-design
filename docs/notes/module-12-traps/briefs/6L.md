# Brief 6L: lesson `nesting` (Module 12, lesson 6), its labels

Read `00-module.md` (the shared fact sheet) first; it applies here. You may use **trap**,
**handler**, **user mode**, **system mode**, **system call** and **interrupt**. Do not write
"nested", "nesting", "reentrant" or "priority". No bold in labels. Headings and labels start with
a capital letter.

Keys to write, each a short label (a title or heading is a few words, no full stop; an objective
starts with a verb and ends with a full stop; a caption is one sentence naming what the figure
shows, with a full stop):

- `title`: the lesson's question: what if a trap comes while the handler runs?
- `objectives.1` to `objectives.4`:
  1. say what a trap inside the handler writes over;
  2. give the control registers just after a trap inside the handler;
  3. keep C1 and C2 before letting interrupts in, and put them back before `resume`;
  4. say why a handler must not fault.
- Section headings (keys `question`, `motivation`, `prediction`, `investigation`, `construction`,
  `failureExperiment`, `explanation`, `generalisation`, `challenge`, `reflection`, under
  `titles.`): a door kept waiting; a trap inside the handler; C2 after the handler faults; two
  traps, edge by edge; the registers inside the wait, worked out; interrupts let in, nothing kept;
  keeping C1 and C2; when to let interrupts in; a wait the door can interrupt; where a trap
  happens.
- `challengeTitles.c1`: the registers inside the wait. `challengeTitles.c2`: a wait the door can
  interrupt.
- Captions:
  - `captions.late`: a handler whose long job keeps the door waiting, in the debugger.
  - `captions.predict`: a handler whose job 2 faults on room 5, listed, with a question about C2.
  - `captions.timeline`: that run from the program's call, edge by edge, with the mode after each.
  - `captions.savedListing`: the handler with job 5 keeping C1 and C2, listed.
  - `captions.answers`: four questions about the registers when the door comes in during the wait.
  - `captions.unsaved`: a job 5 that lets interrupts in without keeping C1 and C2, in the debugger.
  - `captions.saved`: job 5 keeping C1 and C2, in the debugger, with the door's time to choose.
  - `captions.wait`: a handler whose job 5 you change, and its tests.
- `keptTitle`: C1 and C2 kept at 410 and 418.
- `fields.c1`: C1 after the door's interrupt. `fields.c3`: C3 after it. `fields.saved`: the word
  at 418. `fields.c0`: C0 after the door part's resume. (Labels render as plain text: no
  backticks.)
- `waitLabels.1` to `waitLabels.4`: the door opens in the wait, and the program ends after it;
  show 5, wait, show 6, the door open; show 5, wait, show 6, the door shut; a word kept in R8
  across the wait.
