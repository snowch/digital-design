state-machines/construction.rows: A row that gives a bit of the next state a `1` is one AND gate: the state's line AND the row's inputs, with an input the row needs at `0` coming through a NOT gate. A row that leads to IDLE, `00`, has no gate, and a row that reads no input has no gate of inputs.
