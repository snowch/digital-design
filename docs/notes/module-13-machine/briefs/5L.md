# Brief 5L: lesson `capstone`, titles, labels and the challenge's sentences

Read `00-module.md` and `5-shared.md` first. Labels may use CPU, machine code, abstraction, join
and trace. A heading names what its section contains, in a few words, with no full stop. An
objective starts with a verb, is plain text with no backticks, and ends with a full stop. A
caption is one sentence describing the figure, ending with a full stop. A field label is a short
phrase with no full stop.

## Labels

- `title`: the lesson's question: can you write a program of your own and trace its run down to
  the gates?
- `objectives.1` to `objectives.4`: write a program of your own for the shop that uses set if; run
  it on the whole machine; answer questions about one wire at one edge of its run by tracing; say
  what each level hides on the way down.
- `titles.question`: a program of your own. `titles.motivation`: questions only a trace answers.
  `titles.prediction`: MET at the ALU edge. `titles.investigation`: COUT beside MET.
  `titles.construction`: answering from your own run. `titles.failureExperiment`: MET held at 0.
  `titles.explanation`: what each level hides. `titles.generalisation`: the same levels in every
  CPU. `titles.challenge`: your program, traced. `titles.reflection`: from a box to a gate.
- `challengeTitles.c1`: your program, traced.
- `captions.free`: the figure's program on the whole machine, ready to trace.
- `captions.predict`: the same, paused before the ALU edge of its set if, with a question about
  MET.
- `captions.carry`: the same, for COUT and MET at that edge.
- `captions.fault`: the figure's program with MET held at 0, beside the model.
- `captions.cap`: the challenge: your program and five questions about its run.
- `faults.met`: MET held at 0.
- `fields.result`: Y at the ALU edge of your first set if, signed. `fields.carry`: COUT at that
  edge. `fields.met`: MET at that edge. `fields.address`: ADDR at the MEMORY edge of your first
  store, in hexadecimal. `fields.pcBit`: D of the PC's bit 4 at the WRITE edge of your first set
  if.
- `cases.both` etc.: a test's name, one line each, no full stop: `cases.mixed`: room A -184, room
  B -250. `cases.both`: both rooms -250. `cases.neither`: room A -150, room B -100. `cases.edge`:
  room A -200, room B -201. And one per question: `cases.result`, `cases.carry`, `cases.met`,
  `cases.address`, `cases.pcBit`: the field's label again, as a test's name.
