# Brief V5: the words on the lab's drawing of a text's joins

Read `00-module.md` and `4-shared.md` first. These are short labels on a drawing, each a few words,
no full stop, lower case unless a name needs a capital. Keep every `{slot}` exactly as given, once.

## The drawing

Under the lab's text, and in each `lab-run` figure, a drawing shows the joins a text makes between
the course's nine parts. It is read from the text alone, so it follows the text as it changes, and
it is drawn even when the text does not elaborate yet. It never compares the text with the course's.

- **A map**: three frames, one for each block of lesson 1's drawing: the control unit (decoder,
  system, controller, traplogic), the datapath (registers, alu, condition, cregs) and the memory
  port (memory). Each part is a button; under its name it says how many of its ports nothing joins,
  or that every port is joined. A part the text does not place says so.
- **One part's joins**, for the part chosen: the part as a box with its port names inside it. Each
  input port has a wire coming in from the left, labelled with what feeds it: another part's
  output, written `controller.MLOAD`; one of the machine's inputs; a constant, written as the text
  writes it, `1'b0`; or something the text works out in its own logic, written with what the text
  writes at the port. Each output port has a wire going out to the right, labelled with what reads
  it: other parts' inputs, one of the machine's outputs, the text's own logic, or nothing. A port
  the text leaves unjoined is drawn as a short stub ending in a hollow ring.
- In a `lab-run` figure, the ports the changed line feeds are drawn in the accent colour.

## Keys

- `joinsMap`: the name of the map, for a screen reader: the nine parts, by block.
- `joinsBlocks.control`, `joinsBlocks.datapath`, `joinsBlocks.port`: the three frames' headings:
  control unit, datapath, memory port.
- `joinsOpenCount`: under a part's name: {n} of its ports joined to nothing.
- `joinsAllJoined`: under a part's name: every port joined.
- `joinsMissing`: under a part's name: the text does not place this part.
- `joinsPartLabel`: the drawing's name for a screen reader: {part}'s joins.
- `joinsOpen`: the label at an unjoined port: open, joined to nothing.
- `joinsInput`: the label at a port fed by one of the machine's inputs, {name}.
- `joinsOutput`: the label at a port read by one of the machine's outputs, {name}.
- `joinsText`: the label at a port fed by the text's own logic; {text} is what the text writes at
  the port, such as `ADDR` or `IR[31:28]`.
- `joinsReadByText`: the label at an output the text's own logic reads.
- `joinsUnread`: the label at an output nothing reads.
- `joinsNone`: one sentence shown in place of the drawing when the text cannot be read at all;
  {problem} is the reader's message.
- `joinsMarkNote`: one sentence under a `lab-run` figure's drawing: the ports drawn in colour are
  the ones the changed line feeds; a line that feeds no port directly marks none.
