# Brief V5b: one more label on the lab's map

Read `V5.md` first. When a part is chosen, the map marks each other part that the chosen part
joins to: a part whose output feeds one of the chosen part's inputs, or whose input reads one of
its outputs, by the wires the text names. The mark is an accent line on the part's button and a
label under its name and its count.

- `joinsLinked`: that label, a few words, lower case, no full stop; {part} is the chosen part's
  name, once: this part is joined to {part}.
