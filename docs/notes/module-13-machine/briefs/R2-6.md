# Brief R2-6: the shared figure's new words, and two refusals

Strings for Module 13's figure of the whole machine, shared by every lesson of the module, and two
refusals for the answer boxes, shared by the course. Each is a label or one sentence. Keep every
slot in braces exactly once. Plain text, no backticks unless a key says so. Read `docs/style.md`.

## The run strip

Above the drawing, the run's lines are listed, four at a time. Each line shows its address and its
text, then its edges side by side, one button per edge, labelled with the edge's number counted
from the reset and its state (FETCH, READ, ALU, MEMORY, WRITE). The next edge is marked. Pressing an
edge pauses the run before it. Two buttons move the list four lines earlier or later.

- `stripLegend`: the heading over the list, and the name a screen reader gives the group: the run,
  each line with its edges; press an edge to pause before it.
- `stripEarlier`: the button that shows earlier lines (two or three words).
- `stripLater`: the button that shows later lines (two or three words).
- `stripEdge`: an edge's button: `{n}`, the edge's number from the reset, and `{state}`, its state,
  in that order, e.g. "Edge 13, ALU" in shape.

## Pinning a wire

- `pinNote`: one sentence under the drawing: press a wire to pin it; it stays marked as you open
  blocks, and its name and value show under the drawing, in hexadecimal for a word.

## The prediction

- `explainNext`: appended to the verdict after the learner commits, before they have stepped:
  press "Next edge" to see why. (The explanation shows after the next edge.)

## Refusals in the answer boxes (`invalidForm.number`, `invalidForm.hex`)

Shown when an answer box holds something that is not of its form; `{field}` is the box's label.
Say what form the box wants. Never give the answer.

- `invalidForm.number`: {field} wants a whole number, in decimal, such as -12 or 40.
- `invalidForm.hex`: {field} wants hexadecimal digits, 0 to 9 and A to F, such as `02C`.
