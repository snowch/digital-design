question: Lesson 1 ended with a question: what should the machine do with a word that is no instruction?

Kinds 0 and 9 to F have no kind line. A register job has jobs 0 to 7, so job 8 is not a job.

The word `82000005` is kind 8, job 2, constant 5. That job copies a control register into register Y, and the constant says which one.

The machine has five control registers: 0 to 4. There is no control register 5.

How does the decoder tell that a word is no instruction?

motivation: A word the machine must refuse is an **illegal instruction**: a word the decoder does not know.

A word is illegal for one of three reasons:

- its kind is 0 or 9 to F;
- its job is one its kind does not define (jobs 8 to F of kinds 1, 2 and 5; jobs outside 0, 1, 8 and 9 of loads and stores; any job but 0 of calls and jumps; jobs 5 to F of kind 8);
- it is job 2 or 3 of kind 8, and its constant, read signed, is outside 0 to 4.

At an illegal instruction, the decoder gives CAUSED `21`.

Kind 8: job 0 gives `41`, jobs 1 to 3 are handled by a later module, job 4 is `stop`.

Module 9's decoder reads K, J and C.

p1Explain: CAUSED is `00`. The word `84000005` is `stop`, and the machine stops at it with no fault.

Only jobs 2 and 3 of kind 8 name a control register. The check on the number counts only for those jobs.

Job 4 names no control register, so its constant is not read.

The word `82000005` is kind 8, job 2 with constant 5. It gives CAUSED `21`. The figure below shows the checks that reject it.

checksOpenLead: The figure opens the decoder's "checks" block. The instruction is `82000005`: kind 8, job 2, constant 5.

The block has six parts:

- "kind check" outputs NOKIND, 1 when no kind line matches.
- "job check" outputs BADJOB, 1 when the job does not match its kind.
- "number check" outputs BADNUMBER, 1 when job 2 or 3 of kind 8 uses a number outside 0 to 4.
- An OR gate joins these three into ILLEGAL.
- "system jobs" outputs STOP, 1 for jobs 1 to 4 of kind 8.
- "cause" outputs CAUSED: `21` when ILLEGAL is 1, `41` for kind 8 job 0, `00` otherwise.

Press a block to open it. Press the pins for K, J and C to try other words.

checksOpenAfter: The checks read the kind lines, not K. "kind check" is one NOR gate. NOKIND is 1 when no kind line matches.

"job check" has one AND gate per group of kinds:

- kinds 1, 2, 5: job bit 3 (jobs 8 to F);
- loads and stores: job bit 2 or 1;
- calls and jumps: when any job bit is 1;
- kind 8: job bit 3, or job bit 2 and one of bits 1 or 0 (jobs 5 to F).

"number check" reads the bits of C. C is outside 0 to 4 when any of bits 11 to 3 is 1, or when bit 2 is 1 with bit 1 or bit 0.

For `82000005`, C is `005`: bits 2 and 0 are 1. So BADNUMBER is 1, ILLEGAL is 1, and CAUSED is `21`.

With C at `004`, the word is legal, and STOP is 1.
