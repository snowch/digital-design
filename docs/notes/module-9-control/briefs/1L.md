# Brief 1L: lesson `control-signals` (Module 9, lesson 1), the labels

Read `00-module.md` (the shared fact sheet) first; it applies here, except that a label is short
and need not be a sentence. "control unit" is allowed.

Keys to write, each one line. Objectives and captions end with a full stop; headings do not.
Headings start with a capital letter and are short labels, 2 to 6 words, like "In order no
longer" or "The carry, step by step", never the descriptions below. Never use "your".

- `title`: a question a learner would ask, ending in "?", at most 9 words: how the decoder works
  out the control signals from an instruction.
- `objectives.1` to `objectives.4`, each starting with a verb, at most 12 words: predict a
  control signal for a kind; trace a kind's line to the signals it sets; write two signals as
  ORs of kinds; write all twelve signals as a `case`.
- Ten headings, `titles.question`, `titles.motivation`, `titles.prediction`,
  `titles.investigation`, `titles.construction`, `titles.failureExperiment`,
  `titles.explanation`, `titles.generalisation`, `titles.challenge`, `titles.reflection`. They
  hold: the decoder, closed until now; a control unit of gates; a jump's B input; the decoder
  opened; two signals written as text; a wrong gate and a lost line; the decoder's rows as a
  table; one line per kind; every signal written as text; gates, and words that are no
  instruction.
- `challengeTitles.c1`: WRITEY and BCONST, in the style "Memory checks, written".
  `challengeTitles.c2`: the control signals, same style.
- `captions.signalsTable`: each kind's control signals, read off the decoder.
  `captions.predictJump`: predict a jump's BCONST, then check.
  `captions.decoderOpen`: open the decoder's blocks and change K and J.
  `captions.writeWrites`: write the two signals and run the tests.
  `captions.decoderFaults`: choose a fault and run the checks.
  `captions.writeSignals`: complete the `case` and run the tests.
- `options.p1Zero`: BCONST is 0, B takes RB. `options.p1One`: BCONST is 1, B takes the constant.
  Each at most 7 words.
- `faults.op0And`: "orOp0 made an AND". `faults.loadLow`: "LOAD stuck at 0". Return them as given.
- The checks' names, each the instruction's kind and job, at most 5 words, in the style
  "subtract, kind 1 job 3": `checks.subtract` (kind 1, job 3), `checks.addConstant` (an add of
  the constant, kind 2, job 2), `checks.load` (kind 3, job 0), `checks.store` (kind 4, job 8),
  `checks.branch` (kind 5, job 6), `checks.call` (kind 6, job 0), `checks.jump` (kind 7, job 0).
