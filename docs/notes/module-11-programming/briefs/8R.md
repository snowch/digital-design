# Brief 8R: the sentences a failed test adds (Module 11), revised

Read `00-module.md` (the shared fact sheet) first; it applies here. Each text is one sentence, plain
text with no Markdown and no backticks, that a failed test adds after what the program left. It
says what the test needs, never the expected number, and never the answer. Function names are
written as given, with no backticks.

Keys to write:

- `larger` (lesson 3): the display must show the larger of room A's amount above -180 and room B's
  above -200, from two calls of overBy.
- `rangeCall` (lesson 3): R1 must hold how far the reading in R1 lies outside the range from R2 to
  R3, or 0 inside it, with R10 to R14 as they were and a return through R15.
- `rangeRun` (lesson 3): the display must show how far the fridge's reading lies outside 20 to 50,
  and ALARM must be on only when that is not 0.
- `roomsCall` (lesson 4): R1 must hold how many rooms are above their limits, with R10 to R14 as
  they were and a return through R15.
- `roomsRun` (lesson 4): the display must show how many rooms are above their limits.
- `stackWord` (lesson 4): a pop copies a word and adds 8 to R14, and leaves the word where it was;
  give the word, as three hexadecimal digits. (This one may use {actual}, the learner's answer, as
  "{actual} is not that word" at its start.)
- `recursionDepth` (lesson 5): starts "{actual} is not it;" then: a call that finds a room pushes 4
  words, a call for a door that leads nowhere pushes none, and R14 starts at 7C0.
- `farthestRun` (lesson 5): the display must show how many rooms lie on the longest way in from
  the hall, counting the hall.
- `farthestCall` (lesson 5): R1 must hold how many rooms lie on the longest way in from the room in
  R1, or 0 for none, with R10 to R14 as they were and a return through R15.
- `mendOver` (lesson 6): the display must show how many readings are above the limit.
