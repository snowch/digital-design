# Brief 6V: the datapath figure's words, the blocks' labels, and one refusal

Read `00-module.md` (the shared fact sheet) first; it applies here, except that a label is short
and need not be a sentence. PC, "program counter", "fetch" and "branch" are allowed.

These are the words of the figure every Module 8 lesson uses. The lessons' prose already quotes
some of them; those keys are marked FIXED and must come back exactly as given. Draft the others.
Each key is one line. `{name}` in braces is a slot the figure fills: keep every slot exactly.

## The figure

- `instructions`: the heading of the list of instructions a learner can put on the IR bus, in
  lessons where the machine has no ROM yet. At most 5 words.
- `clock`: FIXED "Clock edge". `run`: FIXED "Run until it stops". `reset`: FIXED "Start again".
- `registersCaption`: FIXED "Registers". `register`: the column of register names, one word.
  `word`: the column of each register's 64-bit word in hexadecimal, one word.
  `signed`: the column of the word read as a signed number, one or two words.
  `marks`: the column that marks rows, FIXED "Now". `written`: FIXED "Written".
- `programCaption`: FIXED "Program". `address`: the column of addresses, one word.
  `instruction`: the column of each instruction's eight hexadecimal digits, one word.
  `transfer`: the column of what the instruction does, written as a transfer, one or two words.
  `atPc`: the mark in the row PC names, FIXED "PC".
- `busesCaption`: FIXED "Buses". `bus`: the column of bus names, one word. `value`: the column of
  values, one word.
- `devicesCaption`: the table of the shop's devices, one word. `device`: its first column, one
  word. `display`: FIXED "display". `lamps`: FIXED "lamps".
- `ramCaption`: the table of RAM words, at most 3 words.
- `running`: the status line while the machine runs, saying where PC is: "PC is {pc}." style,
  keep `{pc}`.
- `halting`: the status line while HALT is 1: the next edge stops the machine, and why, `{reason}`.
- `stopped`: the status line after the edge that stopped the machine: it has stopped, and why,
  `{reason}`.
- `reasons.11`: a fetch outside the ROM. `reasons.12`: a fetch at an address that is not a
  multiple of 4. `reasons.21`: an instruction the decoder does not know. `reasons.31`: no memory
  at the address. `reasons.33`: a word not at a multiple of 8, or a byte at a device.
  `reasons.34`: a store to the ROM or to a device that is read only. `reasons.41`: the job
  `call system`, which a later module builds. `reasons.stop`: the instruction `stop`.
  `reasons.later`: a system job a later module builds. Each is a phrase that follows "stops the
  machine:" or "has stopped:", at most 10 words, starting with a lower-case letter, no full stop.
- `stepsHeading`: FIXED "The last edge, step by step".
- `stepsNone`: shown under that heading before any edge: press "Clock edge" first. One sentence.
- `stepChanged`: at a step, the buses that changed at it, `{nets}`. One sentence.
- `stepNothing`: at step 0: the clock has just risen and nothing has changed yet. One sentence.
- `back`: FIXED "Back a step". `next`: FIXED "Next step". `end`: FIXED "Last step".
- `answer`: after a prediction, what the machine did: `{answer}`. One sentence, like "The machine
  gave {answer}."
- `noRegister`: in a prediction of which registers an edge writes, the answer "none". One word.

## A refusal

- `gateInstance`: the words the course uses for one construct when a challenge does not allow it.
  The course's sentence is "This challenge does not use {construct}." and `{construct}` is this
  text. It names the construct and gives a small example in backticks: one module used inside
  another, such as `alu alu1 (.A(QA), .B(QB), .Y(R));`. Lower-case first letter, no full stop,
  like the course's other entries: "a `parameter`", "concatenation with `{a, b}`".
