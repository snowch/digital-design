# Brief 2L: lesson `constants` (Module 8, lesson 2), the labels

Read `00-module.md` (the shared fact sheet) first; it applies here, except that a label is short
and need not be a sentence.

Keys to write, each one line:

- `title`: the lesson's title, a question a learner would ask: how an instruction carries a
  number of its own to the ALU. At most 9 words.
- `objectives.1` to `objectives.4`, each starting with a verb, at most 12 words: read a constant
  job's digits and its constant, read signed; predict the 64-bit word a 12-bit constant becomes;
  write the widening as text; add the selector for the ALU's B input to the datapath's text.
- `titles.question` ... `titles.reflection` (ten keys: question, motivation, prediction,
  investigation, construction, failureExperiment, explanation, generalisation, challenge,
  reflection): headings of 2 to 6 words that tell somebody scanning the page what each section
  holds: (question) numbers no register holds; (motivation) the constant job and its 12 bits;
  (prediction) what `F06` becomes; (investigation) constant jobs to clock; (construction) the
  widening written as text; (failureExperiment) the copied bit and BCONST held at 0;
  (explanation) why copying bit 11 keeps the number; (generalisation) constants and their range,
  with an hour built in two instructions; (challenge) the selector written as text; (reflection)
  what the lesson built and the next question.
- `challengeTitles.c1`: the widening challenge, in the style "Adder, written".
  `challengeTitles.c2`: the challenge that adds the selector to the datapath, same style.
- `captions.predictConstant`: predict what R3 holds, then check.
  `captions.constants`: choose a constant job and clock the datapath.
  `captions.writeWiden`: write the widening and run the tests.
  `captions.constantsFaults`: choose a fault and an instruction, and clock it.
  `captions.hour`: build 3600 in two instructions.
  `captions.writeConstants`: write the selector and run the tests.
- `options.p1Negative`: -250. `options.p1Positive`: 3846. `options.p1Unknown`: X. Each the value
  alone.
- The choices of instruction, each the word in backticks, a colon, then the transfer:
  `instructions.copy` `25003F06`: R3 ← -250; `instructions.add` `22103064`: R3 ← R1 + 100;
  `instructions.and` `201030FF`: R3 ← R1 AND `FF`; `instructions.largest` `250047FF`: R4 ← 2047;
  `instructions.smallest` `25004800`: R4 ← -2048; `instructions.register` `12123000`: R3 ← R1 + R2,
  then a few words saying BCONST is 0; `instructions.half` `25006708`: R6 ← 1800;
  `instructions.double` `12666000`: R6 ← R6 + R6.
- `faults.copyLow`: "The copied bit held at 0" in at most 6 words. `faults.bconstLow`: "BCONST
  held at 0" in at most 4 words. Write 0 as the digit.
